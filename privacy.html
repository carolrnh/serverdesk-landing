Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

ServerDesk is a Discord utility for community operators. We collect only what is needed to run the service.

What we collect

  • Discord guild IDs, channel IDs, and role IDs you configure via /setup
  • FAQ content you provide
  • Billing email and subscription status via Stripe if you pay for hosted access
  • Basic operational logs (errors, command usage counts) to keep the bot reliable

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data
  • We do not mass-DM members or scrape member lists for marketing
  • We do not use Server Members Intent for anything other than welcome-on-join when you enable it

Retention

Guild configuration is kept while your server uses ServerDesk and deleted on written request after cancellation.

Contact

Questions: use the contact on the ServerDesk landing page or reply to your Stripe receipt email.
